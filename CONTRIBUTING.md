## How contributions work

Proposed contributions may be submitted for review — for example a bug report
with a proposed fix, a parsing edge case, or a documentation correction.
Submission does not mean acceptance: the Licensor may accept, reject, modify or
remove any contribution at their sole discretion.

Practical guidance:

1. Open an issue describing the problem or proposal **before** preparing a
   significant change, so effort is not wasted on something that will not be
   accepted.
2. Keep proposed patches small and focused; large rewrites are unlikely to be
   reviewed.
3. Never include copyrighted material you do not own — this explicitly includes
   music recordings, map tiles with restrictive terms, fonts and brand assets.
4. Never include secrets, credentials or personal GPS data in issues, patches
   or sample files.
